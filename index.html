CHAPTER 1 · VERSE 1
धर्मक्षेत्रे कुरुक्षेत्रे समवेता युयुत्सवः।
मामकाः पाण्डवाश्चैव किमकुर्वत सञ्जय॥
dharmakṣetre kurukṣetre samavetā yuyutsavaḥ | māmakāḥ pāṇḍavāścaiva kimakurvata sañjaya ||
Dhritarashtra said: O Sanjaya, assembled on the holy land of Kurukshetra and eager for battle, what did my sons and the sons of Pandu do?

Chapter Essence

Arjuna's despondency upon seeing relatives, teachers, and friends arrayed for battle.
